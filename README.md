# 🏨 Grand Palace Hotel & Resort - Management Dashboard

Nidaam casri ah oo loogu talagalay maamulka hoteellada heerka caalami ah (Modern Hotel Management Dashboard), oo leh sawirro tayo sare leh, falanqeyn maaliyadeed (Charts & Analytics), iyo taageerada luuqadaha **Soomaaliga** iyo **Ingiriiska**.

---

## 🌟 Tilmaamaha ugu Muhiimsan (Key Features)

1. **Naqshad Casri ah & Sawirro Heer Sare ah (Modern UI & HD Photography)**:
   - Sawirro tayo sare leh oo ku saabsan qolalka (Presidential Suite, Ocean View, Penthouse, Luxury Villas).
   - Habka Habeenka iyo Maalinta (**Dark Mode / Light Mode**).
   - Naqshad u habboon kombuyuutarka, tablet-ka iyo moobiilka (**Fully Responsive**).

2. **Xogta Degdegga ah (Real-time Key Metrics & KPIs)**:
   - Heerka buuxsanaanta qolalka (Room Occupancy %).
   - Dakhliga maalinlaha ah iyo kan toddobaadlaha ah (Daily & Weekly Revenue).
   - Tirada macaamiisha soo geleysa iyo kuwa baxaya (Check-in & Check-out trackers).
   - Tirada guud ee martida joogta hoteelka (Active In-House Guests).

3. **Garaafyada Falanqeynta (Interactive Analytics with Chart.js)**:
   - Garaafka dakhliga iyo boosaska toddobaad kasta (Weekly Revenue & Bookings Hybrid Chart).
   - Garaafka noocyada qolalka iyo sida loo kala degan yahay (Category Breakdown Doughnut Chart).

4. **Maamulka Qolalka & Shaandheynta (Room Management & Filters)**:
   - Kala saarid degdeg ah: *Dhammaan (All)*, *Bannaan (Available)*, *La degan yahay (Occupied)*, *Nadiifin ku jirta (Cleaning)*.
   - Raadinta qolalka magac ahaan, lambar ahaan ama nooc ahaan.
   - Beddelidda xaaladda qolka hal gujin kaliya (Quick Check-Out, Mark as Available).

5. **Diiwaangelinta Boosaska (New Reservations Modal)**:
   - Diiwaangeli macmiil cusub, xul qolalka bannaan, dooro taariikhda soo gelidda iyo bixidda.
   - Xisaabin toos ah ee wadarta qiimaha.
   - Dabaaldeg confetti ah marka booska la xaqiijiyo!

6. **Taageerada Luuqadaha (Multilingual - Soomaali & English)**:
   - Hal gujin ku beddel dhammaan qoraallada iyo nidaamka inta u dhexeysa Soomaali iyo English.
